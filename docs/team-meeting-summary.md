## Selected Proposal

**TaskFlow — Team Task Management System**

TaskFlow is a web-based project management application for students and small
teams. It helps users create projects, assign tasks, track progress, and
collaborate through a centralized dashboard.

The proposal was selected to address the difficulty of coordinating
responsibilities, deadlines, and communication across multiple tools. A single
workspace for projects and tasks gives the team a clear view of ownership and
progress.
